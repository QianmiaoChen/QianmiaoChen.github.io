Qianmiao (Michelle) Chen

Research Economist
Development Impact Evaluation (DIME), World Bank

I study public procurement: how the rules governments use to buy goods and services shape competition, corruption, and firm growth. My work combines large-scale administrative data, mostly from China and other developing countries, with empirical and structural methods, and feeds into the World Bank’s advisory work with governments on procurement reform.

I received my Ph.D. in Agricultural and Resource Economics from the University of California, Berkeley in 2024.
